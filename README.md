## 🧠 Ethics and Philosophy

I do not treat software as neutral. Every design choice carries social and ethical consequences.

- **Human dignity first:** tools should help people think and create, not devalue their work.
- **Clarity over hype:** understandable systems are safer, more teachable, and easier to maintain.
- **Reproducibility matters:** if results cannot be reproduced, trust erodes.
- **Open ecosystems:** shared knowledge and community ownership beat black-box dependency.
- **Responsibility in AI:** build systems that augment human judgment, not replace accountability.

I care about the technical result and the human outcome with equal intensity.
